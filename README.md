After four billion years of silently absorbing humanity's wishes, howls, and late-night promises to change, the Moon finally speaks back — with the weary, wry voice of an old rock that has heard it all before and is, frankly, unimpressed. This entry is a single funny comeback line from the Moon itself, delivered in the voice of a celestial body that has seen every broken promise twice and still shows up every night with great lighting.

## Usage

```bash
python3 main.py
```

This writes the Moon's reply to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Entry

> Every night you wish on me, howl at me, and promise you'll change. Four billion years — and I'm still just a rock with great lighting.
